# Final Freedom — Hyper3D checklist (every model)

**57 models.** Do them one at a time, top to bottom. For each one:

1. Upload the **picture** (from the `reference/` folder in the art pack).
2. Copy the **prompt** box and paste it (the style words are already on the end).
3. Settings: **lowest face count** · export **GLB**.
4. Generate. Not happy? Generate again.
5. Save it with the **Save as** name and send it to Claude (attach in chat, or upload to `art/incoming/` on GitHub).

You can send them a few at a time — no need to finish everything first. Tick them off as you go.
**Runner tip:** the pictures show today's simple runner mid-stride. If Hyper3D copies that pose instead of the T-pose, try again with the prompt only (no picture).

Skipped on purpose: the floors and the plain coin (the current ones are fine). The far-away copies (`rock-spire-far`, `tree-far`) and the repeated clouds and gold columns reuse the models below.
