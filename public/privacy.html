Final Freedom — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026 (analytics added)

Final Freedom is a game. It does not ask for your name, email, phone number, location, contacts or photos, and it has no accounts.

What stays on your device

Your progress, coins, shop items, best scores and settings are saved on your phone or browser.

Online backup of your progress (recovery code)

So you never lose your progress, the game keeps a backup copy of it on our server. The backup holds only game data (progress, coins, shop items, upgrades, missions, best scores, game settings, your random player ID and leaderboard name). It is filed under a random recovery code made on your device, shown in Settings → Save & restore. The code itself is not stored on our server, only a one-way fingerprint of it. No account, name, email or other personal information is involved. Anyone who has your code can load your progress, so keep it private.

What is sent to our server (online leaderboard)

Only if you play the Ascended State, your score is sent to our online leaderboard so players can compare scores:

DataWhy
A random player ID created on your deviceTo keep your leaderboard entry yours. It is not linked to your identity.
The leaderboard name you pick from our list (for example "Fearless Halo 83")Shown on the leaderboard. You cannot type a name, so it never contains personal information.
Ascended score, distance, coins and run timeTo rank scores and to block impossible (cheated) scores.

The leaderboard is stored with Supabase, our database provider. We do not sell this data, share it for advertising, or use it to track you.

Game analytics (GameAnalytics)

To find out which parts of the game are too hard, where players stop, and which features are used, the game sends anonymous gameplay events to GameAnalytics, an analytics service for games. These include: how far you got and how a run ended, the journey stages you reached, judgment results, coins earned and spent, abilities and settings used, plus basic device information (device model, operating system, language, approximate country) and a random analytics ID. It does not include your name, email, contacts, location or anything you type. We use it only to improve the game; it is not sold or used to show you ads.

Deleting your data

In the game, open Settings → Delete my online data. This removes all your leaderboard entries and your online backup from our server, and gives your device a new random player ID and recovery code. Settings → Reset progress erases the progress saved on your device. Uninstalling the game also removes what is saved on your device.

Advertising and purchases

This version of the game shows no ads and has no purchases. If optional rewarded ads or in-app purchases are added, this policy will be updated before they appear, and the app store listing will say so.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and older. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
